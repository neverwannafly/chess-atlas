# Privacy notice

Last updated: 2026-10-31

Chess Atlas lists chess tournaments in India and shows results and ratings for the players in them.
This notice explains what personal data we hold, why, where it comes from, and what you can do about it.

## What we hold

- **Player name, federation and club**, as printed in tournament results.
- **Rating** as printed in a tournament (chess-results) or in FIDE's published monthly rating list.
- **FIDE id**, when the tournament's starting list prints it or the FIDE list includes the player.
- **chess-results Ident-Number**: the site's own number for a player, shown on the player card.
- **FIDE title and FIDE rating history**, from FIDE's published monthly rating list.
- **Tournament results**: standings, rounds, pairings and game results.

We do **not** hold dates of birth or years of birth.

## Where it comes from

- Tournament data: chess-results.com (with the operator's permission). Each page links back to the source.
- Ratings and titles: FIDE's published monthly rating list.
- Archive of older tournaments: the chess-results tournament database for India.

## Why we use it

To show Indian tournaments, their results, and players' ratings and tournament history.
We do not sell it or share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

Tournament and result data is kept while the tournament is listed or archived. Ratings are kept as monthly
history. When you ask us to delete your data, we remove it as described below.

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- **Delete** your data from this site.
- **Correct** something that is wrong.
- **Tell you** what we hold about you.

Use the form at `/privacy/delete`. Give your FIDE id, or the chess-results tournament number and your start
number on that event. We reply to your email within 30 days.

After we erase your data, your name is replaced with an anonymous record and your FIDE profile and ratings
are removed. Later imports will not bring them back. Tournament results still show a game was played, under
the anonymous record.

Limits: chess-results.com and FIDE publish their own data. Erasing it here does not remove it from those
sites. Ask them directly for that.

## Who is responsible

Grievance officer details will be published here soon. Until then, send privacy questions and deletion requests
through the deletion form at `/privacy/delete`.

Chess Atlas is in beta. Some details are still being completed, and this notice will be updated soon.

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is
operational under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.
